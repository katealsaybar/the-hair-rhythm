Questions

The honest answers.

Is a junior stylist experienced enough?
She is a specialist in the appointments she does most. Our junior stylists do more blow-dries, more toner appointments and more maintenance treatments than anyone else in the salon, and that repetition is what makes them brilliant at them. Every one of them is trained the Tara Rose way and supervised by senior team members on everything she offers you.
What is a Tone Reset?
Toner and a blow-dry, thirty to forty-five minutes. It resets your tone before the warmth creeps back in, which is why week eight can still look like week one. For blonde and colour clients it is the appointment that makes the most visible difference for the least time in the chair.
How often should I be coming in?
It depends on your hair and your colour, and your stylist writes yours down for you. A common rhythm is colour, then a Tone Reset at week four or five, then a blow-dry or a treatment around week eight, then colour again around week ten.
Does a course really work better than one treatment?
One appointment improves things. A course changes them, because the repair builds session by session rather than starting again each time. Your stylist tracks it at every visit and will tell you honestly if a single session is all you need.
Which salons run The Hair Rhythm?
All four: Khalifa City A and Mamsha Al Saadiyat in Abu Dhabi, and Al Quoz 1 and Motor City in Dubai. A salon appears here only if it can actually deliver the appointment.
What if it does not feel right afterwards?
If something does not feel right within seven days of any colour or treatment service, come back and we refine it. No questions.
