Questions

The honest answers.

Is a junior stylist experienced enough?
She is a specialist in the appointments she does most. Our junior stylists do more roots, toners, trims, blow-dries and maintenance treatments than anyone else in the salon, and that repetition is what makes them brilliant at them. Every one of them is trained the Tara Rose way and supervised by senior team members on everything she offers you.
What is the difference between a refresh and a bigger change?
A refresh keeps what you already have: your roots, your face frame, your tone, your shape. A bigger change moves it: more lift, a new placement, full colour. Bigger changes begin with a consultation with your stylist. Refreshes are what your junior stylist does between them.
How often should I be coming in?
It depends on your hair and your colour, and your stylist writes yours down for you. A common rhythm is a gloss or Tone Reset at week four or five, then roots, a face frame or a trim around week six to eight, with a treatment at any visit and home care in between.
Which salons run The Hair Rhythm?
All four: Khalifa City A and Mamsha Al Saadiyat in Abu Dhabi, and Al Quoz 1 and Motor City in Dubai. A salon appears here only if it can actually deliver the appointment.
What if it does not feel right afterwards?
You are covered by the Confidence Promise, in full, further up this page.
